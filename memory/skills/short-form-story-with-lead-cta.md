## Skill: short_form_story_with_lead_cta

**When to use:**
- Launching the first revenue stream.
- Platforms with high organic reach (Telegram, Twitter, LinkedIn).
- Audience seeking quick, actionable value.

**Steps:**
1. Identify a pain point relevant to the target niche.
2. Craft a concise 3‑5 sentence story that frames the problem and hints at a solution.
3. Offer a free downloadable asset (e.g., blueprint, checklist) that solves the problem.
4. Include a clear CTA linking to a lead‑capture form or affiliate product.
5. Add emojis and a short URL to boost engagement.
6. Post on the chosen channel, monitor clicks/leads, and follow up.

**Pitfalls to avoid:**
- No CTA or broken link.
- Overly long copy; lose attention.
- Using copyrighted images without permission.
- Making false claims or income promises.
- Ignoring platform posting limits.

**Metrics to track:**
- Click‑through rate (CTR).
- Leads captured.
- Affiliate conversions.
