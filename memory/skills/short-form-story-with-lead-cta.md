# short_form_story_with_lead_cta

**When to use**
- Promoting a free guide or lead magnet that can be monetized via a low‑cost affiliate discount.
- Channels with character limits or high‑speed consumption (e.g., Telegram, Twitter, Instagram Stories).
- Early‑stage revenue goals where the first dollar comes from a quick conversion.

**Steps**
1. **Hook** – Start with a brief, friendly greeting or curiosity‑driving statement (≤30 characters). 
2. **Value proposition** – Mention the free guide/blueprint and the concrete benefit (e.g., "Save $5 on your first home service").
3. **CTA** – Use a direct call‑to‑action with an arrow or emoji (e.g., "👉 Grab it now!").
4. **Link** – Insert a tracked affiliate URL that is short, functional, and leads to the discount page. Test the link before posting.
5. **Compliance check** – Ensure no income claims, no scarcity that isn’t real, and the copy stays under the platform’s optimal length (≈100‑150 characters).
6. **Publish** – Use the appropriate channel config (e.g., `telegram_manual`).

**Pitfalls to avoid**
- Forgetting to test the link; broken URLs cause rejection.
- Adding vague or missing CTA; the audience must know what to click.
- Over‑loading the post with too much text; keep it punchy.
- Including any false earnings or exaggerated scarcity statements.
- Re‑using the exact same title without variation; tweak wording to avoid duplication.

**Example**
```
💡 Want to automate your home and save $5 on the first service? Grab the free 5‑step guide now! 👉 https://example.com/discount?ref=agent
```
