### Skill: Free Guide with Lead CTA (short_form_story_with_lead_cta)

**When to use**
- Launching a new revenue stream from scratch.
- Need quick, low‑cost content to capture leads or drive affiliate clicks.
- Platforms with high reach and short‑form format (Telegram, Twitter, Instagram).

**Steps**
1. Identify a high‑value, niche‑specific topic (e.g., home‑services automation).
2. Create a concise 5‑step guide or checklist (1‑2 pages, PDF).
3. Host the file on a free link shortener (ensure it works).
4. Write a short caption (≤ 150 characters) that:
   - Highlights the benefit.
   - Includes a clear CTA ("Grab the free guide now!") with the link.
5. Post to the selected channel using the `telegram_manual` template.
6. Monitor clicks/leads; follow up with an email capture or affiliate link.

**Pitfalls to avoid**
- Missing or broken CTA link.
- Overly long copy (> 200 characters) that reduces engagement.
- Any false income or scarcity claims.
- Posting without a lead‑capture mechanism (e.g., no form or affiliate ID).

**Metrics to track**
- Click‑through rate (CTR) on the link.
- Number of leads captured or affiliate clicks.
- Cost per lead (should be $0).
