## When to Use
- Promote a low‑cost affiliate discount or flash offer via a short‑form story on high‑reach channels (e.g., Telegram, Twitter).
- Goal: generate the first revenue dollar quickly.

## Steps
1. **Identify a concrete discount** (e.g., $3‑$5 off) that is easy to claim and track.
2. **Craft a fresh, attention‑grabbing title** (avoid duplicate phrasing).
3. **Write a concise story (≤150 characters)** focusing on the problem, the discount, and urgency (24‑48h).
4. **Insert a single, tracked CTA link** (use your affiliate URL with a UTM or click‑tracker).
5. **Add relevant emojis** for visual appeal, but keep copy brief.
6. **Review for compliance** – no false income claims, no impersonation, link works.
7. **Submit for human approval** before publishing.

## Pitfalls to Avoid
- Missing or broken CTA link.
- Overly long or vague copy.
- Duplicate titles that may be flagged as spam.
- Any false or exaggerated earnings statements.
- Using untracked or non‑affiliate links.

## Reuse
- Save this template in `memory/skills/short_form_story_with_discount_cta.md` and fill in the discount amount, URL, and channel each time.
